About

From creating works to creating contexts.

Sepehr Sharifzadeh is an Iranian curator, producer and cultural advisor based in Marseille, France. His work focuses on how performing arts are programmed, produced and presented internationally, and who has access to these opportunities.

Originally trained as a playwright, he began his career working across theatre and film, receiving national awards and international recognition, before moving into production and curation. In September 2012, he co-founded NH Theatre Agency, Iran's first international theatre agency, where he served as Artistic Director, connecting Iranian artists with festivals, venues and cultural organisations internationally.

Since then, he has developed festivals, showcases and professional networks, including Re-connect Online Performance Festival, New Narrative Showcase, Micro-theatre Festival and PADA (Producers, Agents and Distributors Alliance).

Having worked as both an independent producer and an advisor to cultural institutions, he is interested in the relationship between artists and the structures that support, fund and programme their work. Questions of access, cultural mobility, representation and inequality inform his approach.

Today, he works with artists on international touring and partnerships, advises festivals and cultural organisations on programming and inclusion, and designs and facilitates professional gatherings. He also gives keynotes and moderates discussions on international collaboration and cultural exchange.

He served on the Advisory Committee of IETM from 2022 to 2026 and is a Salzburg Global Fellow, an ISPA Fellow and a member of The Festival Academy's Alumni Steering Committee.

Focus

His research and professional interests include:

  • International cooperation
  • Access to mobility, visibility, and professional networks
  • Reciprocal exchange between independent artists and institutions
  • Alternative models for touring and collective knowledge
