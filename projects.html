NH Theatre Agency

Founded in September 2012 as Iran's first international theatre agency, NH helps connect Iranian performing arts practitioners with international festivals, venues and cultural organisations. Its work includes touring, artistic partnerships and festival projects such as Re-connect and the Micro-theatre Festival.

Co-founder & Artistic Director · 2012

New Narrative Showcase

Created in 2021–2022 to introduce Iranian contemporary theatre artists to international programmers and producers, the online showcase brought together around 70 artists and more than 90 professionals through showcases, pitches, workshops and a digital catalogue.

Curator & Executive Producer

Re-connect

A volunteer-led online performance festival launched in March 2020 in response to the global lockdown. It presented 45 performances and 20 panel discussions, reaching approximately 7,500 audience members.

Co-founder & Co-programmer

Micro-theatre Festival

A festival in Iran (2015–2019) that provided a platform for artistic experimentation through monthly performances, festival days, panel discussions and lectures across disciplines.
