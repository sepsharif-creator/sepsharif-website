Drawing on 14 years of experience with independent initiatives, festivals and international cultural institutions, I help artists and organisations develop programmes, find partners, tour work internationally and make their working practices more inclusive.

If you are developing a project, festival, artistic programme, international partnership, or simply looking for a conversation around an idea, feel free to get in touch.

Ways of Working

Curatorial & Programme Development

I develop festival programmes, research artists, review programming strategies and help turn initial ideas into new artistic projects.

Diversity, Equity, Inclusion & Access

I review programming and organisational practices, identify barriers to participation, facilitate staff workshops and develop practical inclusion guidelines. Recent work includes co-designing and facilitating a workshop for the European Music Council on diversity guidelines for its events.

Facilitation, Networks & Professional Exchange

I design and facilitate workshops, roundtables, professional meetings, networking sessions and collective learning programmes.

International Touring & Distribution

I develop touring strategies, research festivals and venues, identify potential co-producers, introduce artists to programmers and advise on international mobility.

European & International Cooperation

I develop project concepts, identify international partners and support cultural cooperation initiatives, including Creative Europe projects.
